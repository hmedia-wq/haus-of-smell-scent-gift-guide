When you do not know someone’s scent preference, choose the format first

A botanical fragrance can be a personal gift. The useful first question is not “Which scent is best?” It is how much fragrance the recipient would want, and how they would use it.

Start with permission to scent the space. Someone who enjoys fragrance may still prefer to control when it enters a shared room. If you cannot confidently answer that, the scent-free route keeps the gift within the HAUS OF SMELL catalog without making that choice for them.

A four-question gift filter

  1. Will the gift be used in a shared setting? A fragrance choice affects everyone nearby. If the setting or recipient’s preference is uncertain, begin with scent-free products and let them choose scent later.
  2. Does the person already use the format? A candle asks for a place to burn it and time to watch it. A mist asks the recipient to select a surface and apply it. Choose a format that fits an existing habit instead of inventing one for them.
  3. How much control should they have? A candle is used during a chosen session. A room spray is applied in a chosen amount and location. Neither format is automatically better; the difference is the action the recipient takes.
  4. Is the exact item available today? A category page may describe the line even when no product is currently listed. Open the live catalog, confirm the item and its details, then choose the gift.

What to read before checkout

Use the live product page for the actual size, ingredients, directions, price, and availability. The category descriptions explain the intention of each format, but they do not replace item-specific details. For any scented item, avoid guessing that “natural” means universally suitable; preferences and sensitivities are individual.
